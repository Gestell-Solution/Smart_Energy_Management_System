## 📖 Overview

This document outlines regulatory and compliance requirements applicable to the Smart Energy Management System for residential and small commercial installations.

---

## 2. Electrical Safety Standards

### 2.1 IEC 61010-1

- **Standard**: Safety requirements for electrical equipment for measurement, control, and laboratory use
- **Applicability**: Voltage and current measurement circuits
- **Requirements**:
  - Proper isolation between high voltage (AC mains) and low voltage (control circuits)
  - Minimum clearance: 5mm between AC and DC traces
  - Overcurrent protection (fuses)

### 2.2 IEC 60950-1

- **Standard**: Information technology equipment - Safety
- **Applicability**: Power supply and control circuits
- **Requirements**:
  - Reverse polarity protection
  - Overvoltage protection
  - Proper grounding

---

## 3. Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC)

### 3.1 IEC 61000-6-3

- **Standard**: EMC for residential, commercial, and light-industrial environments
- **Requirements**:
  - Conducted emissions limits
  - Radiated emissions limits
  - ESD immunity

### 3.2 IEC 61000-6-2

- **Standard**: EMC immunity for industrial environments
- **Requirements**:
  - Immunity to electrical fast transients
  - Immunity to surge voltages
  - Immunity to conducted disturbances

---

## 4. Energy Metering Standards

### 4.1 IEC 62053-21

- **Standard**: Electricity metering equipment - AC static meters (Class 1 and 2)
- **Applicability**: Energy measurement accuracy
- **Requirements**:
  - Accuracy class: ±2% (Class 2 acceptable for residential)
  - Metering display requirements

---

## 5. Environmental Compliance

### 5.1 RoHS Directive (2011/65/EU)

- **Requirement**: Restriction of Hazardous Substances
- **Compliance**: Use RoHS-compliant components (lead-free solder, etc.)

### 5.2 WEEE Directive (2012/19/EU)

- **Requirement**: Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment
- **Compliance**: Product marking, end-of-life recycling information

---

## 6. Product Marking Requirements

### 6.1 Required Markings

- CE marking (if sold in EU)
- Manufacturer name and contact
- Model number and serial number
- Electrical ratings (voltage, current, frequency)
- Safety warnings (HIGH VOLTAGE)

### 6.2 Warning Labels

- "WARNING: High Voltage - Risk of Electric Shock"
- "Connect only to properly grounded outlet"
- "Do not expose to water or moisture"

---

## 7. Egyptian Market Compliance

### 7.1 Egyptian Organization for Standardization (EOS)

- Compliance with local Egyptian standards for electrical safety
- Product registration with EOS (if required for commercial sale)

### 7.2 Voltage and Frequency

- **Operating voltage**: 220V AC ±10% (Egypt standard)
- **Operating frequency**: 50 Hz (Egypt standard)

---

## 8. Installation Requirements

### 8.1 Qualified Installation

- Installation should be performed by qualified electrician
- Proper grounding required
- Compliance with local building codes

### 8.2 User Manual

- Clear installation instructions
- Safety warnings and precautions
- Troubleshooting guide

---

## 9. Compliance Verification

### 9.1 Testing Requirements

- Electrical safety testing (hi-pot test, insulation resistance)
- EMC testing (emissions and immunity)
- Accuracy testing against reference meter

### 9.2 Certification (Optional)

- Third-party certification (e.g., TÜV, UL) for international markets
- Egyptian certification for local market

---

## 10. Out of Scope

The following standards are **not applicable** to this residential monitoring system:

- IEC 61850 (Substation automation)
- IEC 62056 (DLMS/COSEM for utility meters)
- Smart grid communication standards
