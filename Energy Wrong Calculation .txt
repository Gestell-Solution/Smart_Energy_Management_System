Summary : System Calculates incorrect Energy 

Expected Behavior: 

   in Full Load line Power Equals nearly 5610w , with simple calculation
   Energy = P * Time 
  0.1kwh = (5610 * Time)/3600000  -> Time = 64 sec 

so system consumes 0.1 KWH every 64 sec


actual behavior : system reads energy in an untraceable way or Hardly traceable 

0.1kwh at 1.5 min (90 sec)
0.2kwh at 4   min (240 sec)
0.3kwh at 7   min (420 sec)
0.4kwh at 10  min (600 sec) although it should be 0.9~1 kwh

 ## Suggested solution : indicating the While loop Frequency to use it as a reciprocal of time (1/time)

note : since the project runs with interrupt system frequency varies

### suggested steps to find the Freq ###

case 1 : Free running Frequency calculation (use dio driver and oscilloscope or logic analyzer) to find frequency of system while sending no commands just the system running in full load (send only 4 commands to turn relays on)

case 2 : Heavy commands send random and as many as possible number of commands while trying to calculate Frequency 

finally : find the average of system freq at cases 1 & 2 and use it to find as the reciprocal of time


Energy = P * time = p*(1/Frequency)
